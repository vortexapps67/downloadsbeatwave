Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

BeatWave is developed by BeatLabs. Your library, cache, habits, and preferences are an offline-first workspace that stays exactly where it belongs — on your device.

1. Data storage & portability

Everything generated or indexed inside BeatWave is saved locally using SQLite/Room. There are no accounts and no login identifiers, and we keep no cloud copies of your collections.

2. Zero telemetry

There are no analytics engines, tracking SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Amplitude), or auto-uploading crash reporters. If the app crashes, records stay on device storage.

3. External connections

  • Streaming: remote catalog queries (e.g. YouTube Music) go directly to public endpoints under their own terms.
  • Listen Together: sync uses Network Service Discovery (mDNS) peer-to-peer over your local network — never a cloud relay.

4. Device permissions

BeatWave asks for local file read access (MediaStore) to map your offline audio, plus local network access for casting. You can grant or deny either at any time.
